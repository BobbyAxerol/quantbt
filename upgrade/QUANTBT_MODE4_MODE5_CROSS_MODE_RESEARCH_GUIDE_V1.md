## 3. Mathematical Objects And Falsifiable Hypotheses

### 3.1 Static Candidate And Temporal Panel

At decision time T, C_T is the frozen finite, feasible candidate roster allowed
by that procedure. F_t are declared time windows; E_t are separately named
market-state views. U_t(theta) is a measured utility with explicit units,
duration and economic preferences. Use the existing utility channel and
reducers; do not mix dollars, Sharpe, PSR and utility in one score.

For static deployment, the primary financial object is one continuous account
with theta held fixed, including its strategy's original internal adaptation.
Attribute original marked observations to calendar/state views. Reset-fold
accounts remain separate temporal diagnostics. Their outcomes cannot be
concatenated or averaged into a static continuous-account equity/Sharpe.
In particular, an archived Mode 4 dictionary used in C_ref needs observations
under the same static account/attribution contract as its competitors before
it can supply b_t. Its original reset-fold utility is not that benchmark.

### 3.2 Near-Optimal Regions And Hypothesis H1

On an explicitly comparable, observed reference panel:

```text
b_t = max_{phi in C_ref} U_t(phi)
G_t(delta_t) = {theta: U_t(theta) >= b_t - delta_t
                     and the declared risk/support conditions hold}
```

Freeze delta, reference scaling, weights and eligibility before the relevant
selection outcomes. Report near-optimal membership, parameter distance to
observed good regions, local downside and forward transfer separately.
Geometry respects categorical branches, inactive axes, log/step/coupled
constraints and effective aliases. An average of winner coordinates is not an
admissible candidate by default; even an admissible medoid must be executed.

**H1, exploratory association:** prefix-measured persistent-region support is
associated with subsequent fixed-dictionary robustness on the declared cohort.
This first design does not identify an independent causal treatment benefit
from region screening. Apparent recurrence due only to overlapping IS, adaptive
sampling density or duplicate candidates challenges the proposed interpretation.
A strict empty intersection is a finite-panel result, not proof that no robust
parameters exist or an automatic no-trade gate.

Stable-region evidence initially explains decisions; it does not change the
selector's gate, ranking or ties. Its proposal-policy use is separately tested
in M5-11.12. Adding a region screen or tie-break to selection would require a
registered treatment revision/ablation before the affected outcomes.

### 3.3 Temporal Regret And Hypothesis H2

```text
r_t(theta) = b_t - U_t(theta)
J_regret(theta) = UpperTailMean_alpha(r_t(theta), weights)
theta_selected = argmin_{theta in C_admissible} J_regret(theta)
```

UpperTailMean takes the worst declared probability mass, including fractional
boundary mass. Candidate admission uses the existing absolute economic/risk
mandate and named support policies. Cash or an approved incumbent can be a
reference, but this guide does not silently replace a NO_SELECTION result with
a deployed cash strategy or impose positive utility in every fold.

C_ref and its measured b_t are frozen for a comparison stage. Expanding the
reference changes the objective: issue a new revision and recompute every
comparable score. Never feed Optuna moving-reference objectives as though they
had one immutable meaning. A hindsight finite-panel winner is not a tradable
oracle or a global optimum.
For new candidates outside C_ref, negative r_t legitimately means beating the
frozen reference; do not clamp it to zero or enlarge C_ref mid-study. State
whether a comparison uses C_ref equal to the common panel or an independent
fixed reference. Reference-relative regret is not full-space regret.

Development and forward references have different clocks. Development b_t
may be measured and frozen before search on permitted development intervals.
Before an outer deployment, freeze C_ref membership and its evaluation rule,
not unknown forward values. Only after that horizon closes, measure its
matched forward reference accounts and calculate b_forward for assessment.
Those outcomes cannot affect the already sealed candidate/recipe; a stored
development maximum cannot stand in for the forward benchmark.

**H2:** controlling the severe shortfalls of a static dictionary improves
temporal/regime balance at an explicitly reported cost in aggregate utility.
The baseline is the existing frozen Mode 5 objective/selector, not a newly
weakened comparator. Compare absolute quality alongside regret to reject
best-of-bad or inactive-flat interpretations.

For common fixed weights/reference:

```text
sum_t w_t * r_t(theta) = constant - sum_t w_t * U_t(theta)
```

Average regret is therefore not a new robustness objective. Max/tail regret
and absolute low-tail utility are different quantities; do not rename one as
the other. Calibrated uncertainty is required before calling a statistic a
lower confidence bound or a probability of robustness.

### 3.4 Transition Diagnostics And Hypothesis H3

Use the existing qualified state-feature provider. Separately report losses,
activity/exposure changes and recovery around registered state transitions.
Monthly/quarterly boundaries are not assumed regime boundaries. Freeze
transition definitions, window widths, overlapping-event attribution, support
and state-fit rights before outcomes.

**H3:** some apparently robust state averages hide concentrated transition
losses or slow strategy adaptation. This is a descriptive, secondary hypothesis
in the first experiment; no transition-penalty optimizer or new classifier is
added. A later selection use would require a new scientific version.

### 3.5 Relative Mode 4 Metrics

Preserve the original matched raw-Sharpe identities:

```text
Q_t = O_t(candidate) - O_t(anchor)
R_t = [I_t(anchor) - I_t(candidate)] + Q_t
```

Ridge's anchor-relative predicted decay is not absolute utility, a cross-fold
universal score or a calibrated probability of basin survival. Keep actual
R/Q, IS contribution, forward quality and economic paths separate. Do not
translate a changed anchor/history into an old Ridge model identity.

## 4. Experimental Design And Registration

The following are proposed bounded defaults for phase review, not approved
execution settings. M5-11.10 must produce concrete manifests with exact dates,
alpha cells, methods, work forecasts and owner-review decisions. No unresolved
TBD field may enter a financial registration. Amendments occur before affected
outcomes; old results remain in their original scope.

| Dimension | Initial bounded design |
|---|---|
| Inventory | All seven existing private alpha entries, read-only |
| Financial roster | At most two compatible alpha/market/account cells initially |
| Candidate support | Reuse the qualified full panels; ordinarily >=128 unique effective feasible points for broad geometry claims; disclose effective count rather than equating attempts to points |
| Selector arms | Existing Mode 5 selector versus one temporal-regret challenger; stable-region/transition diagnostics first remain explanatory |
| Mode 5 sampler arms | Same frozen objective/selector, ordinary proposal policy versus one archive-informed compatible policy |
| Mode 4 sampler arms | Same native selector/objective, original sampler versus one prefix-dossier-informed policy; meta off for the isolation experiment |
| Formal budget | 256 attempted proposals per fresh matched study, seed 731 and the existing documented derivation; no early stopping or outcome-driven extra seeds |
| Mode 4 screening origins | Three fixed existing eligible early/mature/late cutoffs per admitted alpha, selected by calendar/support before arm outcomes |
| Static forward qualification | Proposed three nonoverlapping 365-day deployment horizons per admitted cell, selected by calendar/data availability, not performance |
| Inference | Paired calendar blocks; proposed 95% family confidence, primary family/tail resolution and economic margins explicitly sealed before outcomes |

The static horizons do not redefine Mode 4's original monthly or quarterly
schedule. If the original snapshots cannot support three valid full horizons
with adequate preceding information, record the limitation and seek a scope
disposition. Do not shorten the horizons or use overlapping monthly slices
as extra independent repetitions to meet a count.

Three origins are a bounded procedure experiment, not a power guarantee.
Separate calendar-block uncertainty conditional on the selected paths from
uncertainty about candidate search or procedure performance across origins.
No universal alpha-family claim follows from two financial cells. Keep wider
archive description distinct from that bounded financial evidence.

Formal proposals are counted including rejected/pruned/failed attempts under
the registered policy. Reused 128-attempt studies can inform selector-only
analysis; they are not matched baselines for fresh 256-attempt sampler arms.
Any exact continuation to a new budget requires an expressly registered
study identity and verified complete state, not seed-based reconstruction.

Keep three distinct comparisons:

1. Selector-only: same full candidate panel, comparable observations and
   support. Do not rank only the candidate subset that happened to be labeled.
2. Sampler-only: same objective/selector/admission/budget, different generation.
   Register supplied seeds and screening expenditure; archive priors are not
   free observations. Report incremental and cumulative compute separately.
3. End-to-end: the entire declared diagnostics -> archive -> search -> select
   procedure is prefix-valid. A fixed-bank rerank cannot certify full adaptive
   search or learned environment/geometry construction.

**Input:** reviewed archive/selector outputs and explicit phase approval.
Proposed total ceiling is 4,096 proposal slots for two cells: 1,024 for the
Mode 5 comparison plus 3,072 for Mode 4's three-cutoff comparison. Exact valid
reuse reduces fresh work; this ceiling is not a dispatch instruction.

### 7.1 Mode 4 -> Mode 5

Within the allowed current development scope, derive a finite candidate
seed/prior policy from qualified archive regions. Keep a declared neutral
exploration share, branch coverage and an out-of-archive route. Freeze the
prior's source cutoff, mixture weight, allocation and coordinate conversion
before the search. Do not copy old objective values into the new study.

Use at most one archive-informed challenger to the original proposal policy,
256 attempts each, identical frozen Mode 5 objective/selector and economics.
Supplied/enqueued seeds count in the same budget. Initialization is the

Every new candidate still needs the objective's actual measured inputs under
the same account/interval contract. A full continuous evaluation can provide
multiple lawful calendar views, but unobserved archived forward cells cannot
be replaced by interpolated Ridge predictions or favorable partial masks.
Include that execution and reference-panel cost in preflight, not just the
cost of proposing coordinates.
Use the entire frozen comparison mask for every proposed candidate; prohibit
nearest-region substitution and candidate-specific window dropping. A proposal
may need more than one original account invocation under its declared contract,
so 256 proposals are not automatically 256 executions.

### 7.2 Mode 5 -> Mode 4

At each registered origin, construct or reuse only a dossier whose complete
dependency graph is prefix-permitted. Fit geometry, feature scales, thresholds
and advice within that prefix. Any earlier validation/forward outcomes used
must be mature and permitted; no outcomes of the current forward interval
participate in search, selection or early stopping.

The initial challenger changes only proposal policy using a frozen
compatibility/coverage rule. It does not change Mode 4's native objective,
selector, rolling/expanding schedule or observer acquisition. Keep meta off
in both arms to isolate search. Existing Ridge evidence remains a comparator
in reports; this experiment does not certify Ridge on changed sampler/history
identities. A later meta-enabled comparison needs its own compatible history.

### 7.3 Matched Execution And Interpretation

Use the existing sampler factory, admissible numeric/mixed representations,
sequential ask/tell and exact continuation contracts. One thread per study;
independent studies may run concurrently only after shared resource admission.
Same seed does not imply identical candidate sets across samplers.

Freeze primary comparisons before outcomes. Report effective points,
duplicates, branch support, chronological budget curves, selected utility,
floor/tail, regret, probes and relevant actual forward outcomes. Differences
in data, exposure, objective and selector remain alternative explanations
for bull concentration; no universal TPE-bias claim follows from one cell.

**Outputs:** two direction-specific registrations/ledgers, prefix dossier
references, full attempts/dispositions, optional exact-continuation receipts,
matched sampler findings and qualification limits.

## 8. M5-11.13 Protocol: Whole Procedure And Handoff

**Input:** reviewed prior exits, frozen baseline and at most one supported
hybrid procedure per cell, plus explicit phase approval. A negative earlier
result permits an owner-reviewed no-new-finance disposition; it is not
silently promoted or used to launch a wider recipe tournament.

1. Seal the complete procedure before opening new outer outcomes: candidate
   acquisition, archive cutoffs, geometry/environments, prior, objective,
   selector, uncertainty, fallback and deployment horizon. At each origin
   reconstruct the whole dependency graph using only available information.
2. Reuse exact prefix studies when every procedure dependency matches.
   Otherwise run the registered full prefix procedure, not a final-window
   winner transplanted backwards. The proposed ceiling is 3,072 proposals:
   two cells x three origins x two procedures x 256 attempts. This distinct
   prefix work is not a rerun of an identical completed optimizer.
3. Keep one external parameter dict fixed for each 365-day forward horizon;
   preserve internal strategy adaptation. Execute continuous original accounts
   with declared initial/terminal states and compare paired dates. Different
   dictionaries across origins evaluate the calibration procedure, not one
   dictionary fixed across all years or a stitched live deployment.
4. Perform actual-input future-mutation tests: unavailable market rows,
   labels, revisions, final dossiers, late candidates and reference rows
   must not alter earlier candidate sequence, recipe, model state or selected
   dict. Mutation inside the permitted past may change them. Check outcomes
   and account paths independently, including carry and boundary handling.
5. Assess quality/robustness with declared primary and secondary contrasts.
   Proposed primary benefit is reduced upper-tail temporal regret subject
   to a sealed absolute forward-quality noninferiority margin and risk
   mandate. Report raw forward quality, loss magnitudes, low-tail utility,
   drawdown/recovery, participation, costs, uncertainty and R/Q decomposition
   wherever those relative quantities have valid matched operands.
   Forward regret uses the prefix-frozen reference roster evaluated only
   after the horizon, on the same forward account/calendar contract as the
   competing dictionaries. Count all required reference-account executions.
6. Label old reviewed windows exploratory even after algorithmic no-leakage
   passes. Genuine independent confirmation requires untouched data or a
   future forward period. Maintain alpha-specific outcomes and a declared
   macro summary; time blocks shared across alphas preserve common shocks.
7. Produce shared diagnostics/research-report JSON and readable decisions,
   reconcile the complete M5-11 research coverage ledger, and prepare an
   owner-review handoff to M5-12. Technical completion, economic benefit,
   generalization and production adoption have separate statuses.

The primary whole-procedure qualification here is static Mode 5. Reverse
Mode 5 -> Mode 4 evidence from M5-11.12 remains a three-cutoff sampler screen
plus prefix-isolation proof; it does not certify a complete rolling Mode 4
deployment or changed-sampler meta history. Baseline-versus-combined evaluation
measures total procedure benefit, not an independently identified interaction
between selector and sampler. Do not infer that interaction without its own
matched ablation.

If inference lacks sufficient effective support or tail resolution, retain
INCONCLUSIVE and the exact estimates. An outcome-sensitive new search, new
alpha roster or threshold adjustment is a new protocol, not a repair.

## 10. Shared Architecture, Cost And Cleanup

Reuse the shared sampler/evaluator, typed evidence/access policy, immutable
prepared market, metric reducers, geometry and
[pre-search reports](../docs/diagnostics/PRESEARCH_RESEARCHER_REPORT.md) /
[fold decision reports](../docs/diagnostics/FOLD_DECISION_REPORTS.md).
Extend focused modules and versioned schemas only when a capability is missing;
do not grow another engine, registry, cache or Python/Rust bridge.

Separate data roles, candidate generation, financial execution, numeric
reduction, selection and rendering. Use measured existing Rust/Numba kernels
where appropriate; keep orchestration and cold reports outside the hot path.
No audit dropping, financial approximation or weaker comparison may masquerade
as a performance improvement.

Before every approved execution:

- Register source/artifact/receipt pins, exact target paths and a work ledger:
  reused observations, fresh proposals, unique evaluations, selected/held/stress
  accounts, prefix controls, numeric reductions and original audit work.
- Reuse the current qualified environment/native artifact. Build/install only
  if a newly justified change needs separate admission; it is not a default
  research step.
- Proposed read-only lane: one worker, <=512 MiB owned RSS, <=64 MiB compact
  retained analysis and <=64 MiB scratch. Read large ledgers in bounded chunks.
- Proposed financial ceiling: two independent workers, one numeric thread
  each, <=2 GiB aggregate owned RSS and >=512 MiB RAM available. This ceiling
  is shared with overlapping research jobs; a second controller does not gain
  another 2 GiB allowance. Defer launch rather than preempt unrelated owners.
- Proposed new-series retention cap: 1 GiB total, references rather than copies
  of old accounts; preserve >=5 GiB free disk beyond forecast peak incremental
  use and inspect inodes too. Actual account-size/timing controls and audit
  peaks determine admission before long work, not a single optimistic sample.
- Freeze exact wall/CPU caps, scratch paths and stop/resume semantics in each
  phase registration. A failed cap retains complete checkpoints and failures;
  it does not reduce trials/horizons or extend its own deadline silently.

Independent subagents can review contracts, math/oracles and report/privacy
coverage in parallel. Financial concurrency remains governed by one coordinated
resource/ownership budget. Source-pinned executing files and retained evidence
are immutable; use an isolated versioned lane for any necessary fixes.

Every phase follows [cleanup rules](../docs/BUILD_TEST_CLEANUP.md):
review exact inactive reproducible scratch, retain source/evidence/artifacts,
verify protected hashes after cleanup and report actual free-byte changes.
No broad cache deletion, private evidence purge or unrelated service control.

## 11. Researcher Output And Exit Dispositions

Every phase provides a concise public-safe report and a private detailed
JSON/Markdown appendix, without exporting alpha source/parameters in Git.
Reports include:

1. Hypothesis, baseline, exact method/version and data-use role.
2. Actual work versus registered work, effective points, observed candidates,
   labeled origins, calendar/state support and missingness by reason.
3. Parameter geometry/region recurrence, local downside and uncertainty;
   adaptive sampling density and overlapping IS as competing explanations.
4. Fixed-candidate annual/state/transition quality, low-tail utility, temporal
   regret, net return distribution, DD/recovery, activity/exposure and costs.
5. Native/meta/static decision IDs and dictionary references, explanatory
   components, changed/unchanged outcomes and valid-mask R/Q where applicable.
6. Sampler compatibility versus actual generation/selection/procedure benefit;
   baseline quality and the price paid for robustness remain visible.
7. Source/role/hash lineage, accounting/chronology tests, failures, performance,
   actual RSS/storage/cleanup, claim limits and next bounded action.

Use distinct statuses for technical validity, economic comparison, statistical
support, historical contamination and deployment authority. Legal outcomes
include KEEP_BASELINE, SUPPORTED_IN_REGISTERED_SCOPE, INCONCLUSIVE,
NO_SELECTION, INCOMPATIBLE_SCOPE and NOT_EVALUATED. Technical bugs block their
dependent claims; negative economics does not require retuning until positive.

Completing required registered experiments with honest negative/inconclusive
findings can close research scope. Skipping promised work, inventing a missing
measurement or calling an unqualified procedure complete cannot. Any remaining
scope limitation requires explicit owner disposition before parent/M5-12
acceptance. Phase approval is not permission to self-approve that disposition.

Frozen Ridge V1, legacy Mode 4/5 and public defaults stay as comparators.
An eventual useful kernel/option may be proposed for product integration
after review; research success does not automatically enable it. CI, publication,
public consumer proof and live deployment retain separate approval.
