## Measurement And Missing Witnesses

For every saved development decision, a separate reducer reads qualified raw
IS/forward observations and checks:

`R = (I_anchor - O_anchor) - (I_selected - O_selected)`

`Q = O_selected - O_anchor`

`R = (I_anchor - I_selected) + Q`

All 480 cells are accounted for: 230 have valid observed winners and 250 retain
missing/undefined dispositions. Scored loss support (330 cells in ML-03) is a
different denominator and must not be confused with known selected-winner support.
No label is invented for an unlabeled full-pool winner. Conditional R/Q can be
described on that finite observed panel, not generalized to a full-pool oracle.
No new-family family-confidence interval, accepted noninferiority or continuous
equity is claimed without an admitted complete schedule and support.

## Fixed And Adaptive Policies

The retained fixed policy is V1 origin-sum Ridge with lambda 10 and a causal
refit per origin; fixed regularization does not mean fixed coefficients.
The proposed periodic recipe mechanism has synthetic positive/negative tests:
quarterly boundaries, newly mature matched support, complete calibration blocks,
2% hysteresis and explicit fallback. It has **no actual market update** here:
the initial nominee admission failed. Periodic hyperparameter adaptation is not
silently equated with ordinary causal beta refitting.

More supporting development origins/groups would require a future preregistered
study. Neither opening evaluation losses to choose a model nor retroactively
relaxing complete-block support is authorized by this closeout.
