## Frozen Raw Metric And Account Contract

Authority is [performance.py](../../src/quantbt/metrics/performance.py),
[original-result observation](../../src/quantbt/optimization/meta_selection/observer.py),
[prepared observation](../../src/quantbt/optimization/meta_selection/prepared.py)
and [W3 detached transport](../../src/quantbt/optimization/meta_selection/reactive_transport.py).
`canonical_metric_contract()` binds source-derived reducer/activity hashes plus
trading days/risk-free/sample policy; the inventory records the exact 365-day ID.
Different policies, economics, timing, accounts and ordered universes cannot
silently pool histories through a shared metric name.

- Raw Sharpe comes from the **original aggregate account** `full_report(scope="full")`,
  not a penalized objective, symbol/cluster average, predicted score or stitched-fold mean.
- Sampling prefers returns between last equity marks of UTC days. Zero previous
  equity contributes zero under the original legacy policy; finite samples only.
  If daily returns are unavailable, the existing finite bar/equity fallback applies.
  First-day-to-first-day sampling does not add a synthetic initial-capital return.
- Sample standard deviation uses **ddof=1**. Annualization uses configured trading
  days for multi-day observations; original short-window median-bar fallback uses
  365.25 days/year. Prepared witness requires its qualified daily reducer and
  trading_days=365; do not launder an incompatible reducer into that witness.
- Activity is original report `num_trades`: one initial count per position column
  plus nonzero position differences. It is **not** fill count or completed round trips.
  Prepared `report_trade_count` and W3 original-result witnesses must reconcile
  with that definition; do not relabel package fills as this activity measure.
- VALID requires original verification, finite raw metric, positive first mark,
  at least two samples and positive variance. A finite Sharpe zero with variance
  is valid; flat/no-variance, liquidation/censoring, incomplete and failed outcomes
  retain typed statuses and do not become zero-valued training labels.
- E03-G01 compatibility is explicit `legacy_zero_base_v1`. Native default
  `native_skip_zero_base_v1` remains a different policy; require fails or auto
  observably falls back if the installed wheel lacks the exact capability.
- Canonical `fee_rate` is one-way. Preserve original compatibility conversion of
  legacy round-trip `fee`; legacy meta admission rejects conflicting overrides
  (`fee_rate != fee/2`) rather than changing historical accounting. Costs, funding,
  quantity/tick limits, leverage, liquidation and margin come from the original
  execution contract, never from learner-side assumptions.
- Nominal funding-source policy is family identity; actual eligible prefix values,
  calendar and events bind the market witness. Future funding suffix changes must
  not change an old decision; in-window funding/cost/margin changes must invalidate
  its witness. Delivery legs cannot accrue nonzero perpetual funding.
- Diagnostics start fresh/reset-flat. Final scalar/matrix/package/typed payloads
  execute once in the original continuous account; do not compound separate fold
  equities. W3 remains independently segmented reset-flat and reports that scope.
- Preserve six original execution buffers (equity, returns, positions, fees,
  funding, margin), accepted/rejected order/quantity evidence and aggregate/leg
  reconciliation where supplied. Do not discard V2 margin via a legacy projection.
- Intrabar carried trades retain protection unless explicitly amended; command
  orders survive fold transitions; callback factories are fresh while execution
  state carries. Gap, SL/TP/trailing/session/tick/funding timing stays endpoint-specific.
- Decision snapshot is captured before search/fit. Seal precedes current forward
  work. Cutoff, completion, availability, publication order and effective time
  are distinct; equality is visible only under the existing explicit order rule.

These are documentation locks of existing source, not estimator repairs or a
claim that all routes have complete default-support mutation coverage today.

## Privacy And Artifact Classes

**Public:** only reviewed synthetic fixtures, source/test hashes, capability
contracts and sanitized aggregate reports. Public synthetic params are labelled
as such. A PASS inventory/log is not approval to publish a strategy.

**Private by default:** proprietary alpha/source/notebooks/data, real params,
trial pools/terminal logs, descriptors, model coefficients/artifacts, host paths
and fine-grained fills/trades. These can reveal strategy even without source.
Keep outside Git/wheel/sdist/public CI bundles; export only owner-approved,
reviewed aggregates. Never serialize such material into an exception or this audit.

**Retained evidence:** sealed scientific receipts, exact wheel/sdist/native bytes,
source staging and logs keep their historical access class. Hash verification
does not authorize copying contents into a public artifact. No secret, token,
credential, symlink escape or external file may enter the staged patch.
