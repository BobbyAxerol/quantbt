## Implementation

`AlphaDiagnostics.analyze(..., research=DiagnosticResearch(...))` adds a typed,
versioned `researcher_diagnostics` view. Omitting `research` preserves the
original v1 payload, identity and Markdown. This module works before a mode,
schedule or meta policy is chosen; that does not activate unsupported WFO routes.

Focused owners separate search support, account attribution, source-analysis
projection, interpretation, recipe cards and rendering. Existing calendar and
monetary attribution implementations now have a packaged shared owner;
historical tool imports are aliases. Their non-import AST bodies are unchanged.
No endpoint, financial engine, metric reducer, model or Rust bridge was added.

| Output | Meaning And Researcher Use |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Exact alpha/market/route/economics/schema, metric units, witnesses, availability and allowed view |
| Attempts and response | Ordered terminal dispositions, pruning reasons, budget, original sampler span and observed best-primary curve; observations are not attempted trials |
| Geometry and coverage | Effective duplicates, branch/category support, source fixed/inactive/cast rules, boundaries and constraints; no invented intrinsic dimension or basin volume |
| Participation | Actual close-mark exposure and observed/zero/undefined days; sparse specialization is not automatically degeneracy |
| Contributions | Existing chronological monetary partition, source calendar/state labels, separate positive/loss/absolute daily PnL masses and available costs |
| Source analyses | Actual supplied selection IDs/components/fallback, sensitivity, local centers/probe roles and stress/comparison fields; absent inputs are named |
| Dossier | Original six matrix identities/counts verified by existing owners, without copying matrix data into every report |
| Findings and options | Evidence, units, scope, competing explanation, missing proof and next bounded action; four factory-compatible sampler cards plus explicit researcher selector advice |

Legacy/group TPE, CMA-ES and Sobol keep their existing geometry policies. Cards
do not silently encode categories, remove parameters, apply settings or choose
a universal winner. Bull concentration can have data, exposure, objective,
sampler and selector explanations; it is not attributed to TPE alone. Adaptive
pools remain ineligible for neutral-design association claims. Aggregate Sharpe
cannot become an annual floor, local fragility or a campaign ledger.

## Limits And Handoff

This phase standardizes pre-search evidence and explanation. It does not claim
the added reporting improves decay or discovers a generally optimal sampler.
The producer's declared witnesses are checked, not authenticated against a
malicious producer. Reconstructed prefix exports are not proof that the report
was used in the historical search.

Current-OOS account paths are intentionally absent from prefix advice. Some
optional factor/local/comparison/campaign/opportunity inputs are unavailable or
not joined; reports preserve exact statuses rather than triggering executions.
The original dossier stays the authority for its full matrix values.

Next separate approvals are [M5-11.7.2](../../upgrade/implement.md#mode5-m5-11-7-2)
for fold consistency, R/Q and meta decision views, then
[M5-11.7.3](../../upgrade/implement.md#mode5-m5-11-7-3) for wider endpoint/report
integration and handoff. M5-12, builds, CI, push, merge and publication were not
started. See [cleanup](../maintenance/M5_11_7_1_CLEANUP.md).
