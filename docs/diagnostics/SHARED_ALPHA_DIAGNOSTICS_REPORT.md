## Remaining Boundaries

This is an additive common module, not all of M5-11.7. Rich cross-mode decision,
participation/regime explanation and scientific model comparisons still need
their separately reviewed scope. Geometry/advice does not prove an optimal
sampler for arbitrary unseen alphas. Financial observations remain trusted
producer inputs, not authenticated independent certification. No private alpha,
params, market data or prior evidence is published by this implementation.

Ridge versus other learner families, general hyperparameter policy and any new
loss remain discussion-only. Existing positive and negative economic receipts
keep their original scope. See [cleanup](../maintenance/SHARED_ALPHA_DIAGNOSTICS_CLEANUP.md).
