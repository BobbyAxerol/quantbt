## Account And Metric Ownership

The admitted QuantBT route owns financial execution. Native use requires the
same command tape, clock, lifecycle, fills and account-path differential checks
against the admitted Python owner. Research projection may normalize actual
ledger field identities; it must not create fills, alter inventory or replay a
different financial contract.

MET reporting owns actual net return and drawdown. Canonical Mode 5 statistics
own daily ES, moments and uncertainty. Fees already enter account return and
are not deducted again from utility. The primary mandate remains:

```text
period net return - maximum drawdown - daily loss ES95
```

Quality requires positive primary utility; the frozen maximum drawdown is 0.5
and minimum daily support is 91. Daily support is not sufficient opportunity
support: actual accepted fills, closed/open campaigns and economic exposure
must be shown where the route provides those ledgers. Legacy signal regimes
must not be presented as accepted-fill campaigns.

Annualized geometric growth minus drawdown and daily ES is a separately frozen
descriptive sensitivity view. It never rescues primary eligibility. A leap
year has 366 actual observations; partial 2026 has 278 and is never silently
pooled with complete years. Undefined values remain explicit, not zero.

## Continuous And Reset Views

Continuous-year views retain the actual preceding equity mark as their
baseline. They do not reset inventory or capital. Year-scoped drawdown differs
from a lifetime high-water drawdown. Annual reset rows require actual separate
financial executions, not slicing a continuous path and relabelling it.

Continuous accounts and annual resets also use their declared history
initialization. A full account starts with 2020 preload; a later reset uses its
365-day preload. Their contrast is therefore not automatically an isolated
causal inventory-reset experiment.

## Access And Inference

The selector panel is historical development with reviewed or unknown review
history. Selection across 2021/2025 is retrospective. It is not a causal
parameter decision made at 2021, nor independent whole-procedure validation.
Kept-environment leave-out rebuilds gates from kept environments; it remains a
fixed-panel diagnostic with explicitly disclosed candidate provenance.

Seven-day circular blocks, 256 draws, seed 731 and 95% intervals describe fixed
daily paths. They do not quantify the entire search/selection procedure.
One early/late origin, two candidates and sparse joint directions cannot
support broad transfer or universal economic claims. Two equal-weight
environments with lower-tail mass 0.25 have a tail mean equal to the minimum;
they do not identify the shape of a broad environment tail.

Observed residual inventory, clamp saturation, scarce campaigns, or differing
initialization are mechanism findings, not permission to repair the strategy
or relax the mandate. New outcome-informed descriptive views must disclose
their observation time and cannot change financial requests or selection.
