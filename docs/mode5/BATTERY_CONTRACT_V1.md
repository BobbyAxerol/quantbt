# Mode 5 Empirical Battery Contract V1

This is an offline research contract, not a public Mode 5 activation or a
financial kernel. The [detailed guide](../../upgrade/QUANTBT_MODE5_UPGRADE_V1_DIAGNOSTICS_FIRST_GUIDE_VI.md#49-multi-hypothesis-empirical-battery-giữ-năm-chiều-làm-rõ-mỗi-phép-thử)
and [approved phase](../../upgrade/implement.md#mode5-m5-08) govern its scope.

## Comparison Identity

`ComparisonScope` binds the ordered candidate and environment roster, scored
cohort, target, economic contract and development access policy. A selector-only
comparison reuses the M5-07 canonical selectors on the same actual panel; it
cannot establish a sampler or end-to-end pipeline advantage. Changed cohorts or
prediction targets are `INCOMPARABLE`, not improved decision quality.

The five batteries remain distinct:

| Battery | Question | Required Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Floor resilience on the finite environment panel | Same candidates, risk, quality, costs and exposure views |
| B2 | Fragility under active local probes | Separate selection and held directions; full-schema coverage status |
| B3 | Compromise on a shared Pareto front | Fixed preferences; front hypervolume is not a selector ranking |
| B4 | Fixed-parameter chronological transfer | Forward quality together with raw decay, support and uncertainty |
| B5 | Response to a sampling recipe | Same objective and proposal budget; actual unique work and dispatch |

An insufficient local panel does not receive a SAM selection badge. Two tested
axes cannot establish coverage of an entire active parameter schema. Covariance
cannot silently replace the schema floor. Fixed-panel leave-out is retrospective
development analysis, not unseen OOS or a refitted search procedure.

## Financial And Statistical Ownership

Actual QuantBT execution owners produce account paths and, when available,
accepted fills and closed campaigns. The research layer does not fabricate
orders, edit filled fees to simulate a cost stress, or replay accounting as the
authoritative result. Independent reconciliation checks the actual account.
Signal transitions are not substituted for accepted fills or closed campaigns.

M5-03 statistics, MET reporting observations and M5-07 utility formulas are
reused. Account daily returns include inactive days on the complete UTC calendar.
Daily account returns, campaign outcomes and partial fills have different units
and observation counts. PSR is evidence conditional on its contract, not proof
of future profitability or an adjustment for searching many recipes.

`paired_transfer` reports original daily Sharpe contrasts:

```text
IS quality change      = SR(IS treatment) - SR(IS control)
Forward quality change = SR(forward treatment) - SR(forward control)
Decay change           = IS quality change - Forward quality change
```

Negative decay change alone is not better forward quality. Seven-day circular
blocks share sampled indices between treatment/control within each interval.
The two chronological intervals are distinct samples. Confidence intervals are
fixed-path uncertainty, not whole-selection-procedure or independent-origin
uncertainty. Undefined paths and invalid bootstrap draws remain unavailable;
there is no survivor conditioning or favorable replacement for missing data.

## Risk And Execution Controls

`RiskCalibration` uses permitted, mature train returns only. Its frozen scale
is `min(scale_cap, target_daily_volatility / train_daily_volatility)`. A cap of
one forbids upscaling. The forward account is actually resimulated if scale
changes; an unchanged scale permits exact authorized account reuse.

This is a capped train-volatility control, not a guarantee of equal realized
forward risk. No ex-post scaling of the forward equity curve is allowed. Fresh
strategy/account state is required for every independent scenario. Warm-up,
terminal policies and execution contracts remain explicit and unchanged.

## Search And Attempt Audit

Existing sampler factories and their observation wrapper own RNG and candidate
generation. The audit retains every trial, raw/effective parameters, objective,
status, dispatch and actual work. Calling an independent sampling method can be
normal parameterwise TPE behavior; it does not by itself prove random fallback.
An equal integer seed does not align adaptive sampler proposal sequences.

Proposal count, unique effective candidates, financial calls, visited bars,
cache hits, wall time and RSS are separate quantities. Duplicates do not inflate
coverage. A failed or undefined trial cannot be reported as a completed finite
objective, and cannot silently become an adverse economic score.

`HypothesisLedger` requires registration before observation, with exact scope
and retained evidence references. A post-review recipe, radius or window needs
a new reviewed-development revision. Prior positive, negative and failed
attempts remain retained; copy or persistence cannot forge valid chronology.

## Claims And Handoff

`SupportPolicy` checks preregistered duration, origins, seeds and actual
campaign/fill support. Missing support is explicit. A short diagnostic tranche
can complete its technical work while broad economic claims remain
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. No universal sampler superiority, automatic advisory
deployment, long-term decay reduction or clean-OOS claim follows from it.
