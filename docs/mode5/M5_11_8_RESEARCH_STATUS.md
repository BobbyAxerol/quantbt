similar mean gross risk. Against raw/TPE, it still has greater exposure and
drawdown and a slightly worse minimum annual utility. A positive floor/mean
score does not imply every annual utility is positive.

gate, not a sampler constraint in this protocol: this is an objective/search/
mandate interaction, not evidence that legacy TPE or the selector is always bad.
No incumbent protection or diagnostic warm start was registered; known-point
comparisons remain descriptive rather than silently overriding winners.
