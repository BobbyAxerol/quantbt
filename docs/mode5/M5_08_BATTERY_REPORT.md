## Validity And Resource Accounting

Broad confirmation requires the registered longer horizons, repeated origins
and actual opportunity support. The owner-approved 91-day tranche cannot meet
that claim by construction. Missing full historical trial panels, sparse local
geometry, few sampler seeds and reviewed windows are explicit evidence limits.

The HMA offline compiler requires fresh actual-feedback account passes until
commands, outputs and accepted feedback reach the same fixed point twice. This
is research admission overhead, not one production backtest or a Rust/WFO speed
benchmark. One held-axis cell exhausted 256 passes; its failure is retained and
the identical-input retry converged at 263 after a preregistered technical cap
extension to 512 within the unchanged 900-second timeout. No tolerance, market,
parameter or fixed-point condition changed.

Wall time, unique candidates, execution cells, actual account passes, visited
bars, cache reuse and RSS are reported separately. Early cells without stage
timers are not assigned fabricated retrospective timing components. Successful
and failed work are both retained. Cleanup will remove only reviewed inactive
scratch, after host process/FD inspection; it will not erase research evidence.
