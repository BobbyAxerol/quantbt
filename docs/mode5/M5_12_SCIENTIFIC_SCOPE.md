### Comparison Units And Support

- Mode 4 uses `D = IS Sharpe - forward Sharpe`, `Q = forward_meta - forward_native`
  and `R = D_native - D_meta = (IS_native - IS_meta) + Q`. Each estimand keeps
  its valid mask and original Sharpe convention. Undefined Sharpe is not zero;
  forward need not exceed IS. R alone cannot establish better forward quality.
- For the four-alpha Mode 4 family, registered family inference remains
  **INCONCLUSIVE** because the required calendar/support admission is incomplete.
  Positive descriptive means do not supply numerical family CIs. N1/N2 have
  their own masks; do not pool their figures into that family after outcomes.
- Mode 5 chooses **one fixed dictionary** per arm for the complete declared
  account. Annual/state views attribute that account; they are not independent
  account resets, seven OOS replications or a strategy switching per regime.
- In the reported formal studies, annual `U = net return - DD - daily loss ES95`,
  in decimal-return units, with costs already netted. Other versioned utility
  recipes require their own declared formula. U is not a synonym for return,
  Sharpe or every-year profitability.
- Original Mode 5 cells use BTCUSDT hourly through 2026-10-06 exclusive;
  N1/N2 use ETHUSDT hourly through 2026-10-01 exclusive. Partial 2026 contains
  278 and 273 days respectively. Different markets/calendars/sizing are not
  matched cross-alpha or Mode 4-versus-Mode 5 performance comparisons.

## Finite Robustness, Independence And Whole Procedure

1. **Completed feasible probes are not a complete basin.** The registered tail
   records 534 feasible neighbor observations across eighteen fixed centers,
   center replays and eighteen actual double-fee accounts. Requested
   radius-wide geometry remains `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`; quantization,
   boundaries and coupled directions leave untested coverage. Survival means
   passing the frozen mandate at tested points, not probability of profit or
   a certificate for the whole parameter ball. HMA has no eligible formal winner.
2. **Sensitivity scope is explicit.** Finite-panel association/local probes
   cannot become global Sobol indices by naming. Broader structured sensitivity
   is not certified for every alpha. Absence of a measured effect does not prove
   a parameter structurally inactive or authorize automatic pruning.
3. **Independent arithmetic is not independent economics.** Accounting/oracle,
   selected replay and future-mutation controls establish their specified
   mechanics. They do not erase prior researcher access to the development
   tape, make reviewed data untouched, or turn overlapping state/annual views
   into independent experiments. RSI's unavailable fill/campaign ledger stays
   unavailable; shared-kernel direct controls are not an independent fill ledger.
4. **Cross-mode whole procedure stays deferred.** XMR-031..036 do not obtain
   PASS from the earlier three-cutoff screen or cold disposition. Full learned
   geometry/environment/probe/recipe reconstruction, mature adaptive mutation,
   new 365-day fixed-dict deployments and family inference were not executed in
   that branch. Keep `NOT_EVALUATED / DEFERRED_APPROVED`, not failed research
   and not successful deployment certification.
5. **Source semantics stay source-bound.** Later ACC accounting/configuration
   corrections are intentional. This document does not recompute old research
   with new finance or restore an obsolete claim that financial source never
   changed. Main M5-12 must qualify the actual final source and package pair.
