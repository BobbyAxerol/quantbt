## 1. Executive Assessment

The program has established useful diagnostics, actual-account measurements,
explicit researcher-controlled recipes and bounded positive and negative
comparisons. It has **not** established a universal sampler, selector or
automatic router that improves every alpha, annual environment or market state.
The decision unit is method x direction x alpha x account contract.

The main empirical lessons are:

- A better aggregate Sharpe, terminal equity or tail-regret score can coexist
  with worse annual floors, weaker participation, higher costs or less
  consistent state behavior. These are measured trade-offs, not interchangeable
  definitions of robustness.
- RSI provides both favorable fixed-vector forward observations and adverse
  rebuilt-origin evidence. N1/N2 provide additional counterexamples to choosing
  one recipe or sampler for every alpha. Neither favorable cases nor negative
  cases justify a universal conclusion.
- Same-panel selector comparisons, adaptive objective changes, matched sampler
  comparisons and whole-procedure forward qualification answer different
  questions. The program's stronger reports keep those estimands separate.
- M5-11.10..13 completed their individually approved local scopes. M5-11.13
  explicitly chose **KEEP_BASELINE with no new finance**, rather than claiming
  that its proposed chronological experiments had passed.
- M5-11.9's three remaining formal studies and original-winner/reporting tail
  are under renewed completion authority. Their current terminal results must
  be supplied by the main completion worker; this review does not infer them
  from launch records, historical trial files or software PASS counts.

These conclusions follow from the [RSI decision](M5_11_5_DECISION_QUALIFICATION_REPORT.md),

### Metrics, Mandates And Attribution

For the current utility-based comparisons, `U = net return - maximum drawdown
- daily-loss ES95`, with decimal-return units and the registered scope-specific
drawdown convention. Fees are already in net return and are not deducted twice.
Annual or quarterly U is not additive whole-account U. Positive return need
not mean positive U. The registered current mandate includes finite whole
Sharpe, nonnegative whole U, whole/annual DD at most 50% and annual support of
at least 180 days; it does **not** require positive U every year. Earlier strict
mandates retain their original identities and outcomes.

Raw/TPE uses original whole-account daily Sharpe as feedback and selects the
highest eligible raw-Sharpe candidate under the registered tie rule. It is not
the legacy plateau selector and does not optimize utility. Floor/mean uses
minimum annual U plus 0.25 times its day-weighted annual mean. Pareto/Chebyshev
uses frozen references/scales and its registered preferences; its normalized
score is not directly comparable to another alpha's utility score.
Sources: [RSI mandate and formulas](M5_11_5_DECISION_QUALIFICATION_REPORT.md),
[Raw/TPE clarification](M5_11_9_RESEARCH_STATUS.md) and
[assistance objective definitions](M5_11_12_SAMPLER_ASSISTANCE_REPORT.md).

The mathematical qualification also prevents misleading reinterpretations:
the old floor-minus-weighted-average-regret objective with a common frozen
reference ranks as floor plus weighted mean up to a common constant; it is
not minimax regret. True tail means include fractional probability
mass rather than substituting a quantile; and hypervolume of the same Pareto
front cannot distinguish its selectors. PSR uses the matching period-return
sample and is not a posterior probability of edge or a selection correction.
These are qualified formula/interpretation controls, not additional market
benefits. Sources: [selector oracles](M5_07_SELECTION_REPORT.md) and
[metric/statistical qualification](M5_03_STATISTICS_REPORT.md).

Market-state readbacks reuse prior-closed-bar 168-hour trend, volatility and
market-activity features with .25/.75 thresholds and separate descriptive and
decision-vintage fit namespaces. Full-development fitted labels are descriptive,
not backdatable. Calendar years/quarters are not assumed bull/bear regimes.
Each state family's attributed PnL reconciles separately to the original
continuous account; overlapping families and transition events are dependent
views, not additional trials or independent replications.
Sources: [11.9 state readback](M5_11_9_RESEARCH_STATUS.md) and
[11.11 state/transition definitions](M5_11_11_STABLE_REGIONS_TEMPORAL_REGRET_REPORT.md).

Activity must also keep its producer definition: occupied/close-mark exposure
is not gross notional allocation, fills are not closed campaigns, and zero
close exposure does not rule out intrabar trading. Flat/no-variance Sharpe is
undefined, not a favorable zero. Unavailable campaign or opportunity support
cannot be reconstructed from signal transitions.
See [shared account/reporting semantics](../diagnostics/PRESEARCH_RESEARCHER_REPORT.md).

## 13. Why Baseline Retention And No Universal Auto-Router

The observed reversals are substantive. N1 group TPE lowered DD yet had weaker
held survival and lost capital under actual double fees. N2 Sobol improved its
matched Pareto study but used more risk than Raw. N1 temporal regret improved
its tail statistic while weakening annual/state consistency and cost efficiency.
N2 temporal regret improved many defensive measurements without establishing
quality noninferiority. Archive seeding improved N1 growth/activity but worsened
its frozen annual objective; N2 worsened across that robustness comparison.
Reverse-prefix benefits were heterogeneous or supported by one valid pair.
These are the measured cases in sections 6, 9 and 10, not alpha-name rules.

A universal automatic router would require independent whole-procedure
evidence for its actual decision rule: how diagnostics select objective,
sampler, reference, geometry, thresholds, fallback and final dictionary, all
with permitted information and adequate activity/stress support. Compatibility
cards, successful account execution and fixed-path CIs do not supply that
selection-policy evidence. Neither can an automatic rule be learned from the
same reviewed examples and then called independently validated.
See [product gates](../../upgrade/QUANTBT_MODE5_UPGRADE_V1_DIAGNOSTICS_FIRST_GUIDE_VI.md#79-acceptance-matrix-theo-s%E1%BA%A3n-ph%E1%BA%A9m-kh%C3%B4ng-ch%E1%BB%89-phase)
and [whole-procedure cross-mode contract](../../upgrade/QUANTBT_MODE4_MODE5_CROSS_MODE_RESEARCH_GUIDE_V1.md#xm-04).

The supported stance is diagnostics-first and researcher-controlled: retain
baseline/default behavior unless a separately registered, alpha-specific
comparison clears its full mandate. A negative/inconclusive challenger is a
valid research result, not permission to retune gates after outcomes. Baseline
retention does not prove the baseline globally optimal or independently safe
for deployment; it means replacement has not earned the required evidence.
